**Figure 2. Divergence patterns across mitochondrial and nuclear gene modules reveal differences in evolutionary constraint among mitochondrial-associated gene classes.** Distributions of dN, dS, and dN/dS are shown for (A) mtOXPHOS, nuOXPHOS subunit, and OXPHOS assembly-factor genes; (B) genes partitioned by OXPHOS complex and gene class; (C) cytosolic and mitochondrial ribosomal proteins; (D) cytosolic and mitochondrial aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases; (E) mtOXPHOS, nuclear core, and nuclear noncore OXPHOS subunits; and (F) direct n-mt, indirect n-mt, and non-n-mt genes. Boxes indicate medians and interquartile ranges, and whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range. Points represent individual genes; points in the genome-wide Kuster panels were subsampled for visualization, whereas boxplots and tests used all eligible genes. Dotted lines indicate genome-wide medians across 17,965 quality-eligible nuclear genes. Invariant genes were retained with dN=dS=0; dN/dS analyses included only identifiable estimates supported by at least one expected synonymous substitution. Pairwise differences were evaluated with two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests and Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Asterisks denote adjusted P < 0.05 (*), P < 0.01 (**), and P < 0.001 (***).
